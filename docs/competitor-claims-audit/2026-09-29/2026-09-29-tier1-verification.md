## Competitor-by-competitor notes

### Fresha — highest C&D exposure
Daisy’s compare page asserts **no AI**, **English only**, and **UAE-only GCC**. Fresha’s own properties contradict all three: AI Concierge is a priced add-on; Arabic is live (2025 blog); GCC teams extend past UAE. Pricing floor and card rates are also stale ($9.95 / 2.19%). Marketplace **20% one-time new-client** commission is accurate if qualified. **Priority: rewrite before any paid distribution of this page.**

### Booksy
Pricing structure (Biz/Biz+) and “routing-only Digital Doorman” language are the main risks. Booksy’s live **AI Receptionist** page claims real booking, 24/7, English/Spanish (beta; deposits/NSP limited). Arabic/GCC absence claims remain supportable from public pages checked.

### Vagaro
Base price, +$10 calendars, and ~220k professionals are fine. Absolute “AI Receptionist: Not Available” conflicts with Vera branding. Prefer capability-accurate wording (chat/SMS + booking link vs Daisy voice+pay).

### Boulevard
“No native AI receptionist” is fair. Mislabeling **Duo** as an AI assistant and locking “AI” to a stale **$295** Premier price are the problems. Refresh list prices to the live Essentials/Premier/Prestige cards.

### Mindbody
“AI Receptionist: Not Available” is the critical miss—Mindbody sells AI Concierge on Ultimate (add-on Accelerate). Replace $139–699 ladder with “from $79; higher tiers quoted.” Update marketplace metric to **3M+ monthly shoppers**. Commission claim is solid with the 20%/cap nuance.

### GlossGenius
**Reception by GlossGenius** makes “AI Not Available” and “AI analytics only” indefensible. $24 / $148 / 2.6% are largely fine. Soften team-management claim (Gold covers smaller teams).
