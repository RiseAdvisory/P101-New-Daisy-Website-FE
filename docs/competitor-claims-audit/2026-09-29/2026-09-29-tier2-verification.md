## Cross-cutting notes

1. **Own-source standard:** Where Daisy cites numbers rivals do **not** publish (Zenoti $225, RepeatMD $700), label as **estimate / third-party / user-reported**, never as the rival’s price list.  
2. **AI absolutes are the #1 legal/accuracy landmine** in this set (Zenoti count, Phorest, Acuity, SimplyBook, Square, Mangomint). Prefer “no 24/7 multilingual AI receptionist / no cashback marketplace” over “no AI.”  
3. **GCC geography:** DINGG multi-country pages make Daisy’s “1 GCC country” claim the clearest geographic error.  
4. **Pricing currency date:** Mangomint’s Aug 2026 repricing is the clearest stale-price Critical. Timely seat math is second.  
5. **Treatwell 35%:** Verified but must keep “first marketplace booking / 0% repeat” to avoid Defamatory-risk.
