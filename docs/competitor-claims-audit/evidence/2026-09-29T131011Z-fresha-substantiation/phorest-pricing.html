Have a question? Here are
some answers.

Is my information safe and secure?

Very safe. We encrypt your data and securely store it in the cloud. So even if your system fails, your client records and appointments will be safe and available to view when you log in on another device. Our support team are always on hand to help you with access issues and are hyper-vigilant to ensure they get owner consent before making any access changes.

Do you offer a free trial?

Phorest offers comprehensive and personalised set-up of your software, with import of your clients, staff and products, as well as full training for you and your staff. We know you’re busy, so we don’t offer a free trial and expect you to work all that out for yourself. We can, however, take you through a full demo of the product at a time that suits you, so you can see if it meets (and exceeds!) your needs before you buy.

There are so many great features, how can I make sure I’m making the most of them?

Only Phorest salons have their own personal success hero! Your Business Advisor is at hand to help you with your marketing and growth and can help guarantee you return on your investment with us.

Can you set up the software for me?

Yes, of course! All of our plans include bespoke set up of the software for YOUR salon. We’ll add and link your services, staff, rooms, equipment and products; and we’ll give you and your staff the training and support you need to get up and running. We’ll also add the online booking widget to your website and Facebook page.

How easy is it to switch from another provider?

So easy! We’ll do all the heavy lifting for you. Our migration specialists have years of experience in safely migrating client record information from most other salon software; as well as from pen and paper records! All you need to do is give us access and we’ll bring your client data across and set up your staff, services and product lists.
