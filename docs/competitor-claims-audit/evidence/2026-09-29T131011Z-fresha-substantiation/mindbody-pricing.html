Frequently Asked Questions

Is it easy to switch to Mindbody from my current software?

Yes, switching to Mindbody is designed to be easy and seamless. We offer a data transfer service that can move your existing setup, client information, and other data from your previous system, so you don't have to worry about manual data entry. Our team is here to help you every step of the way to ensure a smooth transition.

What support is included? Is there a setup fee?

Every customer gets dedicated one-on-one support at no additional cost. This includes onboarding and training from day one so you launch quickly and confidently, with a system built around how you run your business.

Support doesn't stop once you're live either. Our AI Assistant provides instant, around-the-clock answers whenever you need them—no waiting on hold. If you'd rather learn at your pace, our Learning Center offers on-demand tutorials and trainings, and our support center has step-by-step instructions for every feature. Prefer to speak with a person? You can also reach our team by phone, chat, or email.

There's no setup fee on any Mindbody plan.

What is the Mindbody app? Does it have fees?

The Mindbody app is an exclusive marketplace available to Mindbody customers on all software plans. It provides businesses a marketing channel seen by 3M+ consumers searching for fitness, beauty, and wellness services—exposure and reach no other software provides.

Listing your business on the Mindbody app is free. If an existing client books through the app, came through a tracked referral link, or identifies you as how they found your business, there's no fee for their booking. A fee applies only when the app delivers a client you wouldn't have received otherwise. The fee is 20%, capped at $30 (or 30 in local currency), and only on the first purchase for someone new to your business. Every booking after the first is commission-free. This is a fraction of what most businesses typically spend to acquire a new client through paid marketing, and unlike ad spend, you only pay when it results in a booked client.

Does Mindbody offer payment processing?

Yes, Mindbody offers integrated payment processing available on every plan through Mindbody Payments. Businesses that use Mindbody Payments see advantages with seamless client checkouts, card-on-file transactions, industry-leading security, and payment features built to grow and protect revenue like automatic recurring membership charges and enforceable cancellation policies.

Mindbody Payments is not required. You can use Mindbody software with a separate payment provider of your choice as well.

If you choose to use Mindbody Payments, your rate is based on the Mindbody plan you select. Talk to our team for custom pricing which can include hardware discounts and dedicated payment specialist support.

Does Mindbody offer email and text messaging?

Yes, booking confirmations and reminders by email and text are included on the Accelerate plan and above.

Want full email and text marketing campaigns? The Ultimate plan includes automated email and text marketing, with a monthly text bundle. Need more? Additional texts are a fraction of a cent (or local equivalent) each, so scaling up your marketing never means a surprise bill.

What additional services are available?

Beyond your base subscription plan, you can add extra services as your business needs them, such as a branded app, your own custom app with your logo and colors, to drive repeat bookings and higher client spend. Businesses with a branded app see 36% more bookings year over year and 59% higher client spend after a year.* For larger or growing operations, we also offer premium implementation, custom data conversion, and dedicated Technical Account Management. These are priced separately from your plan, so you only pay for what you add on.

* No guarantees. Results vary.

Is my data safe and secure?

Yes, data security is our highest priority. We employ numerous redundant data safety systems and follow strict PCI compliance procedures to ensure that all of our subscribers' data, including your clients' credit card information, receives the highest possible level of protection.

Will you ever look at my data? Or share it with anyone?

Please reference our Privacy Policy for a detailed outline of if, how, and when we collect, use, and disclose subscriber business data and/or personal information.

Can someone set up the software for me?

Yes, to get the Mindbody software up and running quickly, consider our Technical Account Management services. Our experts work with you to reduce your manual work and find the package and services that help your business grow.

How many logins are included in my plan?

You get unlimited users per location. Unlike other software, you aren't charged per user—we don't charge more as you build your team. Your subscription price increases based on adding new locations and not adding new people to existing locations.

Which Mindbody plan is most appropriate for my business needs?

Most businesses get the best value from Ultimate, since it covers your needs today and grows with you. The right plan for your business depends on two things: your business size (small to medium-sized vs. multi-location enterprise) and your growth goals (basic tools vs. marketing and automation).

Mindbody offers three plans:
 

  • Starter gets your business up and running fast with booking, scheduling, and payments, plus a customized website included in your plan.
  • Accelerate upgrades your day-to-day operations with booking, scheduling, payments, advanced reporting, and operational controls.
  • Ultimate optimizes your growth with booking, scheduling, payments, advanced reporting, operational controls, marketing automation, and analytics.

Our recommendation: Ultimate. It gives you the most value up front plus room to grow, without needing to upgrade plans later.

How long does Mindbody setup and onboarding typically take, and which steps are handled by my team vs. Mindbody?

We help you launch quickly and confidently. Your exact 'go-live' timeline can be shorter or longer based on a few things, like how quickly you can meet with us, share your setup details, and provide any data to move over. More complex businesses—multiple locations, lots of staff, or detailed pricing and services—may need extra time.

Here are things that Mindbody handles for you:
 

  • Software configuration based on your business needs, including data migration.
  • Tailored training sessions built around your day-to-day workflows.
  • Access to self-serve tools like the Learning Center and AI Assistant, as well as webinars and training recordings which you can access anytime.

Here are things your team handles:
 

  • Sharing your business basics like services/classes, pricing, and booking policies so we can set up your software.
  • Providing client lists and any other data that needs to be transferred.
  • Training your staff (but we also give you lots of self-serve options like Learning Center certifications, webinars, and support articles).
Am I locked into a contract with Mindbody? What is the process for cancellation?

It depends on your plan and billing terms, including any promotions you signed up for.

Cancellation is usually handled in your account or through Customer Support, and may require advance notice based on your agreement (for example, canceling before your next billing date.)
