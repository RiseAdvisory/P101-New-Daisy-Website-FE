Reception answers your calls and texts 24/7 and books clients in seconds for you. So you never lose another booking, can stay fully present with your clients, and can unplug after hours.

  • Handles customer questions
  • Books appointments directly into your GlossGenius calendar
  • Explains your services and pricing
  • Follows your deposit, card on file, and cancellation policies
  • Adds clients to your waitlist
  • Get clients in sooner when applicable
  • Handles multiple calls at once
​

Yes. Unlike most AI receptionists that text clients a booking link, Reception actually books appointments onto your GlossGenius calendar in real time — just as if you added it yourself. It sees your services, prices, staff schedules, and open slots so there are no double bookings and no manual entry. If your policy requires a deposit or card on file to book, Reception takes care of that as part of the booking.

​

Yes. Reception books by your rules. It collects a deposit or card on file when your policy requires one, and every client hears your cancellation policy before booking. If someone later tries to cancel inside your window, Reception never charges a fee on its own. It sends the request to you, so you decide whether to charge or waive it.

​

Yes. Reception moves or cancels existing appointments via call or text whenever customers request it, and follows your business's cancellation and rescheduling policies. If a cancellation would trigger a fee under your policy, Reception informs the client before proceeding.

​

Reception doesn't let the call end at "sorry, we're full." It offers the next available time, checks whether another provider on your team is open, or adds the client to your waitlist so they're first in line when a spot frees up. Your calendar stays full, and the booking stays with you instead of going to whoever picks up next.

​

Reception goes live in just a few minutes. It already knows your services, pricing, staff, and calendar, so there's nothing to re-enter and nothing to train.

​

Yes. Reception is on 24/7, including evenings, weekends, and holidays. Because 50% of bookings happen after hours, Reception makes sure your clients can still book, reschedule, or cancel instead of hitting voicemail and calling a competitor down the street.
