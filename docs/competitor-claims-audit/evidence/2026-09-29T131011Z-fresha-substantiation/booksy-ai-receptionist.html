Frequently asked questions

I don't have a salon or barbershop, it's just me. Can I still use this feature as a solo provider?

Yes! It's just you running the show, and we know it's time you got a little bit of help. This feature is accessible and available for you to turn on. You decide when it's handling calls for you — when you're busy with a client, when you're closed for the day, or have it on all the time so clients can always use your phone booking system.

‍

‍

Do I need to change my phone number?

No. We'll give you a dedicated Booksy Phone Number, and you choose how to use it — even both at once:

  • Forward your existing number to it whenever you want AI Receptionist to pick up your calls, like when you’re busy with a client or closed for the day — turn forwarding off anytime. Clients call the same number as always, your receptionist picks up the call and completes the booking for them. 
  • Give clients the Booksy number directly so they call it instead of your personal line. Clients can call to make an instant booking through this line at any time, without waiting for a callback.

Either way, your existing number stays yours and unchanged.

Is this available right now?

AI Receptionist is in beta. Current Booksy providers can request access and start using it today. It's still being built, so expect it to get better fast.

What languages does it support?

English and Spanish. Your system can easily switch to the caller's preferred language to complete the booking.

Can it book clients who need a deposit or have a strict cancellation policy?

Not quite yet. Appointments with No-Show Protection enabled aren't supported in this first version, but will be supported soon.

What happens if it can't handle a request?

It'll flag the call for you to follow up on, by sending you a message with details and a callback request, so nothing falls through the cracks.
