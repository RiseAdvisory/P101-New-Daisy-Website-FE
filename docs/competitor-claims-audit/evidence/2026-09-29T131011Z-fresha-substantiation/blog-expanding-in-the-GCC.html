Fresha continues to expand across the GCC

Fresha

Last updated on July 1, 2025

Published July 1, 2025

At Fresha, our mission has always been clear: to build the world’s most powerful and accessible platform for the beauty and wellness industry. And we’re proud to say that last month, we took a bold step forward in that mission: Fresha is live in Arabic.

This milestone marks more than just a language update. It’s a statement of intent. A commitment to the millions of professionals and consumers across the GCC and wider Arabic-speaking world. With our platform now available in Arabic, we’re making it easier than ever for businesses and clients in the region to connect, manage, and grow with Fresha.

 

 

Growing our presence on the ground

We’ve significantly expanded our footprint in the GCC. Alongside our established teams in Dubai and Saudi Arabia, we’re now launching local operations in Qatar, Oman, and Kuwait. This means more hands-on support, deeper local insight, and stronger relationships with the professionals who power this vibrant industry.

From salons in Doha to spas in Muscat and barbershops in Kuwait City, Fresha is equipping businesses across the region with the tools they need to thrive, in their own language, and with regional support teams that understand their unique needs.
 

Opening doors across the globe

Language is more than words. It’s how we build trust, communicate value, and create lasting relationships. By offering our full platform in Arabic and building Arabic-speaking teams across the region, we’re opening the door to new conversations, new opportunities, and a deeper connection with the beauty and wellness community throughout the Middle East.

And this is just the beginning. We’re excited to continue investing in the region, building partnerships, and empowering more professionals with the tools they need to succeed.

And we’re just getting started 🚀
