concrete claims only, no invented facts). Limit-bound fields sit on single

booking site, 50 included AI conversations, 0% commission on existing clients,

### Description (EN, shared with Google Play full description)

The Daisy is a booking and payments app for beauty and wellness businesses and the professionals who run them.

FOR SALONS, SPAS, AND CLINICS

Run the business from one place: a branded booking site, staff calendars, payments, client records, and reviews. An AI receptionist answers your customers on WhatsApp, Instagram, and your booking site at any hour, so missed messages stop turning into missed bookings. Every plan includes 50 AI receptionist conversations.

Your existing clients stay yours. When they book through your site, your link, or your own social channels, Daisy takes 0% commission. Commission applies only when the Daisy marketplace brings you a new customer.

Automated reminders reduce no-shows. Prepaid packages and cancellation policies protect your revenue. Earnings reports show you how the business is doing.

FOR SOLO PROFESSIONALS

Working alone? Create a professional booking site, manage your appointments, accept payments, and keep client records in one app. The AI receptionist handles booking questions while you are with a client.

The app works in English and Arabic.

Start with a 14-day free trial. No card required.
